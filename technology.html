What is Technology?

Technology is the practical application of scientific discoveries, and it is used to describe new and old inventions as well as anything humans have made using science. It is important to understand technology so that you can better use it and make your own discoveries!

What is the Internet?

The internet is often greatly misunderstood by the vast majority of people who use it everyday. Vint Cerf, a co-creator of the internet, addresses the confusion by explaining the net's history, principles, and design. You can find this video, among others on technology, at code.org.
