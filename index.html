Discover STEM through fun!

The word "episteme" is ancient greek for knowledge or understanding. STEM—the acronym for Science, Technology, Engineering, & Mathematics—requires both knowledge and understanding to connect abstract ideas with real world applications. With the epiSTEMe website, elementary and middle-school students can use a variety of STEM resources, games, and trivia to better understand the growing world around them.
